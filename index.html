Four 4 TB drives: RAID 5 gives 12 TB (75%) and survives 1 failure. RAID 6 gives 8 TB and survives 2.

What it does

  • Usable space for RAID 0, 1, 5, 6 and 10 side by side
  • Failures each level survives, guaranteed and best case
  • Hot spares and the minimum drive count per level
  • TB and TiB, so the number matches your operating system
Rules from the Wikipedia standard RAID levels article. File system overhead not subtracted. RAID is not a backup. Static, client-side, nothing stored. Source
