RaidPick

How much space do you really get from your drives, and how many can fail?
Rules from the standard RAID level definitions (Wikipedia, "Standard RAID levels"). Usable = RAID 0: all drives, RAID 1: one drive, RAID 5: drives minus 1, RAID 6: drives minus 2, RAID 10: half. Minimum drives: RAID 0 and 1: 2, RAID 5: 3, RAID 6 and 10: 4 (RAID 10 needs an even count). All drives are treated as the smallest drive's size, so mix sizes by entering the smallest. "Guaranteed" is the failures any combination survives. RAID 10 can survive more only if each failure hits a different mirror pair. Drive TB are decimal (1 TB = 10^12 bytes) and an operating system that shows TiB reports about 9% less. File system overhead is not subtracted. RAID is availability, not a backup. Runs in your browser, nothing stored.
